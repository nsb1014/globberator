# Game animal part coverage

This pass prioritizes recognizable animal silhouettes used in creature and farm games. Each row links an archetype to reusable parts and lists the distinct additions from this pass. IDs name parameterized parts in the Blender catalog.

| Archetype | Reuse from the catalog | New drafts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dragon or wyvern | `wing_bat`, `horn_sweep`, `crest_dorsal`, `tail_flexible`, `talons` | Swept cheek web and terminal tail spade |
| Unicorn or pegasus | `foot_hoof`, `mane`, `tail_tuft`, `wing_feathered` | Single spiral forehead horn |
| Griffin | `wing_feathered`, `beak_hooked`, `talons`, `paw_padded` | Existing families cover the wing, beak, and feet |
| Minotaur | `head_bovine`, `horn_sweep`, `foot_hoof`, `hand_basic` | Reuses the cattle head and existing humanoid parts |
| Cattle | `head_bovine`, `horn_sweep`, `tail_tuft`, `foot_hoof` | Four-quarter udder |
| Pig | `muzzle`, `ear_pinna`, `foot_hoof`, `tail_flexible` | Rooting disc with recessed nostrils |
| Chicken | `beak_hooked`, `leg_bird`, `wing_feathered`, `tail_feather_fan` | Single comb and paired wattles |
| Horse, donkey, sheep, or goat | `foot_hoof`, `mane`, `ear_pinna`, `horn_curl`, `horn_sweep` | Select hoof split, pinna length, and horn shape for the animal |
| Dog or wolf | `ear_pinna`, `muzzle`, `paw_padded`, `leg_digitigrade`, `tail_flexible` | Square-stop dog muzzle and folded hanging pinna |
| Cat | `ear_pinna`, `paw_padded`, `whiskers`, `tail_flexible` | Paired whisker pads |
| Rabbit | `ear_pinna`, `leg_jumping`, `tail_tuft` | Elongated hindfoot with four low digits |
| Pet bird | `beak_hooked`, `wing_feathered`, `foot_grasping`, `tail_feather_fan` | Reuses the existing beak, wings, and feet |

The fantasy parts use an original clay design sheet. The research ledger cites the natural structures used as references for the spiral relief, tail vane, and membrane folds. Domestic parts use specimen images, diagrams, or breed standards. Each proposal records its closest active family and what the new part contributes.

Inspect the drafts by exact ID with `describe --part` or `library --part`. Their fit, likeness, and applicable motion gates are tracked in the [qualification manifest](qualification.json).
