# Hummingbird model review

The reference guided the rise of the body and bill, the sweep of the wings, and the tucked feet. The far wing and underside were inferred. The model exaggerates the eye slightly and groups the iridescent plumage into broad green and blue regions with shallow feather accents.

- **Wing placement.** Both roots were moved higher and farther forward on the thorax. The shoulder surfaces are seated 1.9 mm into the evaluated body. Front, diagonal, and side views show the raised roots and the original wingtip positions.
- **Foot anatomy and pose.** Each foot has three small forward toes and an opposing inner hind toe. The short tarsus folds upward and rearward in the default hover pose. Profile and underside close-ups show the hock transitions and the orientation of the toes. Each foot pivots independently at the hock. Rotating the reimported foot bone by 40 degrees lowers the foot while leaving its pivot fixed.
- **Export.** Blender reimported the GLB with 11 bones, vertex colors, and 172,176 triangles. The welded geometry had zero nonmanifold edges and a maximum extent of 15.46 cm. Godot 4.7.2 instantiated the asset and confirmed both leg-to-foot bone hierarchies. Eight clay views and the color preview were rendered from this export.

## Independent visual review

A fresh reviewer inspected the color render, clay turnaround, and foot close-ups. The hover pose and high wing sweep read clearly. The far wing is hidden from one side and visible in the rear and oblique views. The shanks and rounded hocks read bulky in close-up, and toe overlap makes individual curls hard to distinguish in profile. The underside shows both sets of curled toes and slight rippling ahead of the tail. These details remain open for a future geometry pass. The source photo was unavailable to the reviewer, so this pass assessed the rendered anatomy and pose.
