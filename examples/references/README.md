# Creature design references

These eight clay studies were generated specifically for a fresh build of the example set. Each recipe started with an empty body plan and used its study as the shape reference. The previous source recipes are in the [archive](../archive/pre-reference-rebuild/).

| Example | Design study | Shape landmarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Minotaur](../minotaur.json) | [Minotaur study](minotaur.jpg) | Broad horn sweep, deep chest, heavy arms, bent legs and cloven hooves |
| [Bull](../bull.json) | [Bull study](bull.jpg) | High shoulder hump, dropped head, broad muzzle and short, strong legs |
| [Bird](../bird.json) | [Bird study](bird.jpg) | Short hooked beak, layered folded wings, upright fan tail and perching feet |
| [Spider](../spider.json) | [Spider study](spider.jpg) | Domed abdomen, small forebody, thick upper legs, tapered feet and paired fangs |
| [Hopper](../hopper.json) | [Hopper study](hopper.jpg) | Rounded lizard silhouette, two feelers and a single bent leg above a broad foot |
| [Crab](../crab.json) | [Crab study](crab.jpg) | Wide shell, eye stalks, prominent pincers and six supporting legs |
| [Dragon](../dragon.json) | [Dragon study](dragon.jpg) | Raised head, long neck, spread bat wings, four clawed feet and tapering tail |
| [Beetle](../beetle.json) | [Beetle study](beetle.jpg) | Rounded shell, rising central horn, small antennae and six articulated legs |

Each study shows one view. Hidden surfaces and the geometry inside joints were inferred. The samples share the solid construction method. Rounded, overlapping segments carry rigid weights, so each segment keeps its shape when its joint rotates.
